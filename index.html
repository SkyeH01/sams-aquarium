Forty years in Bergvliet.

Sam's opened in Bergvliet in 1985 and has grown into the largest freshwater fish and aquarium supplier in Cape Town. The people on the floor keep tanks at home too, so whatever you ask, chances are someone here has tried it.

Freshwater is our speciality: tropicals, cichlids, bettas, goldfish, koi, live plants and shrimp. Reef keepers are welcome too. We carry Red Sea systems, salt, live rock and the rest of the marine kit, and we're always glad to point you to the right thing.

How regulars shop now.

It starts with a message, and we do the walking around.

  1. 1

    Send us a message

    Tell us your tank size, what's living in it already, and what you have in mind. A photo of the tank helps more than you'd think.

  2. 2

    We go and look

    We'll come back with what's in this week and what would suit your tank. Happy to put something aside for you until you can get here.

  3. 3

    Come and collect

    We'll bag your fish for the trip, sort out anything you still need, and talk you through settling them in at home.

What a tank gives back.

A tank earns its place in the room, and it gets better the longer you have it.

In the first month

  • Water that has found its balance
  • Fish that come to the front when you walk past

  • A routine that takes ten minutes a week

A year in

  • Fish grown into their full adult colour
  • Cuttings to trim back and pass on to friends
  • Something the whole house stops to watch
  • People at Sam's who know your setup
