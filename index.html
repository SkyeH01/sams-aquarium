The shop on Main Road.

Sam's opened in Bergvliet in 1985 and has grown into the largest freshwater fish and aquarium supplier in Cape Town. Our staff can help with any setup, from a first tank to a large planted display.

We specialise in freshwater: tropicals, cichlids, bettas, goldfish, koi, live plants and shrimp. We also stock marine equipment, including Red Sea systems, salt and live rock.
