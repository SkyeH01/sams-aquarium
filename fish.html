What's in the tanks.

We restock every week. This page lists what we usually carry. For this week's stock, ask us on WhatsApp.

Ask what's in this week

Reserve fish on WhatsApp.

Tell us what you're after and we'll check the tanks and hold the fish for you. No deposit needed. Please let us know if your plans change.

All our livestock is freshwater, but we stock marine equipment, from Red Sea systems to salt and live rock. See the marine kit.

Community tropicals

Peaceful, colourful fish that are easy to feed and suit most tanks. Ask us which species school, how many to keep together, and which ones mix well.

  • Neon and cardinal tetras
  • Guppies, platies, mollies, swordtails
  • Danios and rasboras
  • Barbs
  • Corydoras
  • Bristlenose plecos
  • Gouramis
  • Rainbowfish
Ask about community fish

Cichlids

Cichlids need the right tankmates and the right water. African rift lake species need hard, alkaline water, and South American species prefer it softer. Tell us about your tank and we'll suggest a group that works.

  • Malawi mbuna and peacocks
  • Tanganyikans
  • Angelfish
  • Rams and apistogrammas
  • Kribensis
  • Oscars and larger South Americans
  • Discus, when we have them
Ask about cichlids

Bettas and nano fish

Small fish for desk tanks and small setups. A small tank needs a heater, a filter and a few weeks to settle before fish go in. We can help you set one up.

  • Bettas
  • Endlers
  • Chili and other dwarf rasboras
  • Ember tetras
  • Pygmy corydoras
  • Sparkling gouramis
Ask about nano fish

Goldfish and cold water

Goldfish grow large and can live for over ten years, so tank size matters. Ask us what size suits the fish you want.

  • Orandas
  • Fantails and ryukins
  • Black moors
  • Comets and shubunkins
  • White cloud mountain minnows
Ask about goldfish

Koi and pond fish

Koi and pond fish, plus the pumps, filters and UV sterilisers to keep a pond clear. We'll match the equipment to your pond's volume.

  • Koi
  • Pond goldfish
  • Pond pumps and filters
  • UV sterilisers
  • Pond food and treatments
Ask about koi

Live plants

Kept in our plant tanks. Tell us about your lighting and we'll suggest plants that suit it, from low-light Anubias to carpeting plants that need CO2.

  • Stem plants: Ludwigia, Rotala, Hygrophila
  • Anubias and Java fern
  • Cryptocorynes
  • Mosses
  • Carpeting plants
  • Floating plants
  • Substrates, fertilisers and CO2
Ask about plants

Shrimp, snails, loaches and plecos

Shrimp, snails, loaches and plecos help keep a tank clean. Some graze algae and others eat leftover food. They're useful alongside regular water changes.

  • Neocaridina shrimp
  • Amano shrimp
  • Nerite and mystery snails
  • Loaches
  • Plecos
  • Otocinclus
