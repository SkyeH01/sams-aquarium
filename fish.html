Freshwater specialists

What's in the tanks.

Rows of freshwater tanks, restocked every week. This page covers what we usually carry. For today's list, ask us on WhatsApp.

Ask what's in this week

Reserve before you drive.

Message us what you're after. We'll check the tanks, let you know what's in and looking its best, and put it aside for you. No deposit needed. A quick note if your plans change is all we ask.

Keeping a reef? The livestock in our tanks is all freshwater, though we carry the marine side of things too, from Red Sea systems to salt and live rock. See the marine kit.

Community tropicals

The backbone of most tanks, and a pleasure to watch. Peaceful, colourful and easy to feed. Ask us which ones like to school, how many of each looks best together, and who makes a good neighbour.

  • Neon and cardinal tetras
  • Guppies, platies, mollies, swordtails
  • Danios and rasboras
  • Barbs
  • Corydoras
  • Bristlenose plecos
  • Gouramis
  • Rainbowfish
Ask about community fish

Cichlids

Big personalities with firm opinions about company and water. The African rift lake fish like it hard and alkaline, while the South Americans prefer it softer. Tell us what you're running and we'll find you a group that gets on.

  • Malawi mbuna and peacocks
  • Tanganyikans
  • Angelfish
  • Rams and apistogrammas
  • Kribensis
  • Oscars and larger South Americans
  • Discus, when we have them
Ask about cichlids

Bettas and nano fish

For desk tanks and small setups. A little tank can be a lovely thing given a heater, a filter and some patience while it settles. We'll help you put one together.

  • Bettas
  • Endlers
  • Chili and other dwarf rasboras
  • Ember tetras
  • Pygmy corydoras
  • Sparkling gouramis
Ask about nano fish

Goldfish and cold water

Goldfish are proper pets. They grow to a good size, they learn who feeds them, and a well-kept one will be around for well over a decade. Ask us about tank size and we'll set you up for the fish it's going to become.

  • Orandas
  • Fantails and ryukins
  • Black moors
  • Comets and shubunkins
  • White cloud mountain minnows
Ask about goldfish

Koi and pond fish

For the pond outside. We'll help with the water side too, since a happy pond is mostly good filtration: pumps, filters and UV sterilisers matched to your volume.

  • Koi
  • Pond goldfish
  • Pond pumps and filters
  • UV sterilisers
  • Pond food and treatments
Ask about koi

Live plants

Kept in water in our plant tanks. Tell us about your light and we'll point you to plants that will thrive under it, from an easygoing Anubias to a carpet worth the effort of CO2.

  • Stem plants: Ludwigia, Rotala, Hygrophila
  • Anubias and Java fern
  • Cryptocorynes
  • Mosses
  • Carpeting plants
  • Floating plants
  • Substrates, fertilisers and CO2
Ask about plants

Shrimp, snails, loaches and plecos

The crew that keeps the glass and gravel tidy. Some graze algae, some tidy up leftovers, and some just look good doing very little. A fine addition alongside your weekly water change.

  • Neocaridina shrimp
  • Amano shrimp
  • Nerite and mystery snails
  • Loaches
  • Plecos
  • Otocinclus
