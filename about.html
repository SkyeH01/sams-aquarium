Sam's Aquarium, since 1985.

A freshwater fish and aquarium shop on Main Road in Bergvliet, Cape Town.

From 1985 to now.

Sam's Aquarium opened in Bergvliet in 1985. In 2021 the shop moved to the Caversham Centre on Main Road, where it is today.

The range has grown over the years. The shop now has rows of freshwater tanks, a separate plant section, and shelves of equipment from nano filters to Red Sea reef systems.

Before we recommend fish, we ask about your tank, so what you take home suits it.
