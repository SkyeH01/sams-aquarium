Since 1985

Forty years in Bergvliet.

Sam's Aquarium opened in Bergvliet in 1985. The address and the sign have changed since. Not much else has.

From 1985 to now.

In 1985 Sam's Aquarium opened in Bergvliet, in the Southern Suburbs of Cape Town. In 2021 the shop moved to the Caversham Centre on Main Road, where it is now. Since then it has picked up 777 Google reviews and plenty of regulars.

What's changed: the range. The shop has grown into the largest freshwater livestock and aquarium supplier in the city, with rows of tanks that customers keep describing in reviews as spotless, a plant section of its own, and shelves of equipment from nano filters to Red Sea reef systems.

What hasn't: someone will ask about your tank before they pick up a net. It's the part of the job everyone here enjoys most, and it's why customers who first came in as children now bring their own.

How we sell fish.

Freshwater is our speciality

Our tanks are all freshwater, and we know that side of the hobby inside out. We carry marine equipment for the reef keepers among our regulars, and we're always glad to point you in the right direction.

The right fish for your tank

If a fish you have set your heart on would struggle in the tank you have, we'll say so, then help you find one that will thrive in it. It works out better for everyone that way.

Advice is free, always

Message us, call us, or come in and ask. There is no such thing as a silly question here, and we would much rather answer it before you buy than after.
