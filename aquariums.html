Equipment.

Filters are rated by tank size, but the number of fish matters more. Tell us your tank size and what you keep, and we'll recommend a filter.

Internal filters
For small and medium tanks. Simple, quiet, easy to clean.
External canister filters
For bigger tanks and heavier stocking. More media, less clutter in the tank.
Sponge filters and air pumps
For shrimp, fry and breeding tanks. Gentle and nearly free to run.
LED lighting
Choose by what you keep. Plants need stronger light than fish.
Heaters
Tropical fish need 24 to 26 degrees all year. An indoor tank in Cape Town can drop several degrees on a winter night.
Chillers
For warm summer rooms, and for cold water species that prefer things cooler.
UV sterilisers
For green water and persistent cloudiness, in tanks and ponds.
Test kits and treatments
Ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH. Dechlorinators, bacteria starters, medications.
Food
Flake, pellet, frozen and live. Species-specific where it matters.
Substrate, rock, wood and ornaments
Gravel, sand, planted substrates, driftwood, rock and ornaments.
Automatic feeders and timers
For holidays and busy weeks.
