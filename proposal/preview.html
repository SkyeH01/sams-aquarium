In detail

What is included.

Set up once

  • An online shop matching the website, built for phones
  • Order online, then collect at the counter, or same-day delivery across the Southern Suburbs. Live fish are collected at the counter.
  • A courier account set up and linked to the shop, so an order anywhere in Cape Town is a printed label and a collection, not a phone call
  • Card payments through Yoco
  • WhatsApp set up for the business, with a sign-up so customers choose to hear from you
  • Facebook and Instagram set up properly
  • Your Google listing overhauled
  • Your products set up to appear in Google searches
  • Two training sessions and a written guide, so your team adds products, photos and prices themselves. I am on hand for the first batch.

Every month

  • One WhatsApp message a week to your customers
  • Facebook and Instagram posted every week
  • Google advertising run and adjusted as the results come in
  • The shop, the website and your Google listing looked after, and reviews answered
  • Help with any online order that needs it
  • One report in plain English

What I would need from you

  • A WhatsApp number
  • One person on the floor who handles online orders
  • A way to deliver locally: a staff member with a car, or Uber. The courier covers the rest of Cape Town.
  • A few hours after training to load the first products
