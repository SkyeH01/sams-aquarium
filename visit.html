Before you come

Four things that make the trip go better.

Message first if it's a specific fish
Stock changes daily. Tell us what you're after and we'll confirm it's in and put it aside, so you know it's waiting when you arrive.
Bring a photo of your tank
One photo answers ten questions. If you have test readings, bring those too.
Bring a cooler bag in summer
An insulated bag keeps the water temperature steady on the drive home, which your fish will appreciate on a warm Cape Town afternoon.
Fridays close for lunch
We close between 12:00 and 14:00 on Fridays. Every other day we're open straight through.

Ask us from the couch.

Most questions get answered on WhatsApp the same day. Stock checks, water questions, what to buy, or just what's looking good this week.
